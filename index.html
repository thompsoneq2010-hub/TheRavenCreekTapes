About the film

Six teenage friends set out to document the disturbing history of Raven Creek, a long abandoned town surrounded by local legends, unexplained disappearances, and rumors of a cult connected to a nearby asylum.

What begins as an attempt to uncover the truth soon becomes a struggle to understand what really happened there.... and what is going to happen to them. As the group ventures deeper into the woods and investigates the remains of the town, suspicion begins to spread between them.

Someone knows more than they're letting on. The footage captures things nobody can explain, and the friends begin to question not only what might be out there, but whether they can trust the people they've called friends as they find themselves isolated together.

The cameras were meant to document the mystery. They may be the only things left to tell the story.
